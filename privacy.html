FOLDPOP

PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FOLDPOP.

FOLDPOP is a daily reflection application that allows users to discover meaningful messages and explore their thoughts through an optional AI-powered conversation feature called Go Deeper.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use FOLDPOP, including its reflection features, subscription services, and customer support.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on how you use FOLDPOP, information may include:

  • Messages and reflections you write.
  • Questions submitted through Go Deeper.
  • Conversation content shared with the AI service.
  • Messages you choose to save.
  • Information voluntarily provided when contacting support.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your reflections unless you understand and accept the associated privacy implications.

3. AI-Powered Reflections

FOLDPOP includes an optional feature called Go Deeper.

When you use this feature, your daily message and the conversation content necessary to generate a response may be transmitted to an external AI processing service.

This information is used to generate contextual responses to your reflections.

AI-generated responses are intended for personal reflection and general informational purposes. They are not professional medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice.

The identity of the AI provider, its applicable data handling practices, and any required user consent must be verified and disclosed before FOLDPOP is publicly released.

4. Saved Messages and History

FOLDPOP allows users to save daily messages and revisit reflection history.

Some information may be stored on your device to support these features.

Deleting the application may remove locally stored information.

Any information processed by external services may be subject to separate retention periods.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

FOLDPOP offers optional monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions through FOLDPOP+.

Subscription purchases, billing, and renewals are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit services.

FOLDPOP does not require users to enter payment card information directly into the application.

Subscription status and transaction-related information may be processed to provide access to purchased features.

6. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

  • Provide daily reflection features.
  • Generate responses through Go Deeper.
  • Maintain saved messages and conversation history.
  • Verify subscription access.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.

7. Third-Party Services

FOLDPOP uses Apple services for subscription purchases and management.

Go Deeper may involve an external AI provider that processes the content submitted for generating responses.

Information is shared with service providers only as necessary for the relevant feature, subject to applicable privacy requirements and user permissions.

Details of any additional analytics, advertising, or other third-party services must be disclosed if such services are integrated into FOLDPOP.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored messages and reflections may remain on your device until they are deleted or the application is removed.

Information processed by external providers may be retained according to their applicable policies and contractual terms.

To request information about data associated with your use of FOLDPOP, or to request deletion where applicable, please contact our support team.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing of your personal data.

You may also have the right to object to certain processing activities or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact FOLDPOP Support.

10. Children's Privacy

FOLDPOP is not specifically designed for children.

Users should not submit personal information about children through the AI reflection feature.

If you believe information relating to a child has been submitted inappropriately, please contact us.

11. Security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through FOLDPOP.

However, no electronic transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to FOLDPOP, its services, or applicable laws.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
